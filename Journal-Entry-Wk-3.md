1. **PIR Sensors (Passive Infrared Sensors)**:
   - Detects motion and radiant infrared heat emitted by human bodies in close proximity.
   - how this can be used: Triggers audio as soon as a visitor reaches their hand toward a specific ceramic piece.

2. **IR Thermopile Sensors / Grid Thermal Sensors (need to do more research)**:
   - Measures non-contact surface temperature and heat signatures at precise distances, helpful if we want to keep the ceramic piece intact.
   - how this can be used: Allows for dynamic audio modulation, example: increasing sound volume or changing audio pitch as the hand gets physically closer and transfers more heat toward the sensor.
